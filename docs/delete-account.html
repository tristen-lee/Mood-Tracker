How to delete your account

You can permanently delete your Cairn account and all associated data at any time, directly from the app:

  1. Log in to Cairn
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm when prompted

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, the following is permanently removed:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your password (hashed)
  • All mood check-in entries
  • Your medication list
  • All analytics and history
  • Jasper conversation summaries
  • Push notification subscriptions
